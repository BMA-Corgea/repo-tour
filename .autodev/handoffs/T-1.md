## What to build next, in order

1. **Stage 5 — roll up** (`src/rollup.ts`). Bottom-up, hash-keyed: files → directories →
   subsystems → repo. Each tier written from the tier BELOW, never from source — that
   property is criterion 5 and it is what makes "too big" a non-problem.
2. **Incremental driver.** `git diff --name-status <old>..<new>` against the stored
   manifest. The content-hash keying it rests on already works; what is missing is the
   diff walk and ancestor invalidation.
3. **Stage 4 — interpret.** The only paid stage. Do not start it before 1 and 2: the whole
   design is that free stages narrow the field first.
