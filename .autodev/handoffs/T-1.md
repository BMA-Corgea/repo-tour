## Traps already known

- **Nested git repos.** GUTS's parent tracks 399 files; the real system is 2,505 across
  6 repos. A `git ls-files`-at-root scanner returns a confident wrong answer. Acceptance
  criterion 1 exists for exactly this.
- **Length ranks a repo backwards.** GUTS's five largest files are benchmark exhaust
  (38,367 lines, 1 commit, 0 importers); its most important file is `manifest.yaml` at
  96 lines with 134 commits. Weight LOC third, behind churn and in-degree.
- **The drift-budget constants are invented.** They ship behind config, never inlined,
  until a spike measures them. Do not let them harden into facts.
- **The import graph underdelivers on service-shaped repos** — 124 edges across GUTS's
  ~1M lines, because its organs talk over HTTP contracts, not imports. v1 must state its
  own coverage rather than present a thin graph as complete.
